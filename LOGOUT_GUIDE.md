# How to log out of the Okghumo WhatsApp assistant

## Log out

Send **one** of these as a message on its own:

- `stop`
- `unlink`
- `logout`
- `log out`

Capital letters don't matter, and you can put a `/` in front (for example `/logout`).

The message must contain only that word. Extra words or punctuation, such as "please logout" or "stop.", will not log you out. The assistant will treat them as a normal question.

## What happens

1. Your WhatsApp number is disconnected from your Okghumo account.
2. Your scheduled reminders and reports, and any unsent ones, are deleted.
3. The assistant forgets your recent chat.
4. You get the reply: **"Logged out ✅"**

If it can't log you out right away, it will say so and nothing is deleted. Wait a minute and send the command again.

Your Okghumo business data is never changed or deleted.

## Log back in

Send **hi**. The assistant replies with a link that works for 10 minutes. Open it, log in to Okghumo, then come back to WhatsApp and press Send.
